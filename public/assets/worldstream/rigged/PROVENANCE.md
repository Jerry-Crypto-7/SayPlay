# Riggované postavy – původ

Generuje `scripts/assets/rigged/build.mjs` ze souborů, které stáhl `scripts/assets/rigged/fetch.mjs` (sha256 zdrojů v `scripts/assets/rigged/fetched.json`). Úpravy: jen namapované klipy, textury vložené do GLB (vnější nad 512 px zmenšené), kořenový uzel na podstavu a výšku 1,8 m. Geometrie ani klipy se nemění.

- **KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0** – Kay Lousberg, CC0-1.0, https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0: `knight.glb`, `barbarian.glb`, `mage.glb`, `rogue.glb`, `rogue-hooded.glb`
- **KayKit Character Pack: Skeletons 1.0** – Kay Lousberg, CC0-1.0, https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Skeletons-1.0: `skeleton-warrior.glb`, `skeleton-minion.glb`, `skeleton-mage.glb`, `skeleton-rogue.glb`
- **Kenney Mini Characters** – Kenney, CC0-1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters: `boy-a.glb`, `boy-b.glb`, `boy-c.glb`, `girl-a.glb`, `girl-b.glb`, `girl-c.glb`
- **Kenney Cube Pets** – Kenney, CC0-1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/cube-pets: `pet-penguin.glb`, `pet-cat.glb`, `pet-dog.glb`, `pet-polar-bear.glb`, `pet-bunny.glb`
- **Kenney Graveyard Kit** – Kenney, CC0-1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit: `graveyard-ghost.glb`
- **Kenney Blocky Characters** – Kenney, CC0-1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters: `blocky-robot.glb`

CC0 nevyžaduje uvedení autora; uvádíme ho z vděčnosti.
