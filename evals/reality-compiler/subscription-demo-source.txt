Fictional museum demo, not a real security system.
Corridor grid: width 10, height 3, cell size 1, x=0..9, y=0..2. No internal walls or obstacles. All cells are walkable and all entities non-solid.
Actor visitor starts at (1,1); sensorA at (2,1), sensorB at (8,1), display case at (9,1). Walkable markers approachA at (1,1), approachB at (7,1).
Sensor activation requires visitor in an orthogonally adjacent cell, never remote or on the sensor cell. Zones: nearA={(1,1),(3,1),(2,0),(2,2)}, nearB={(7,1),(9,1),(8,0),(8,2)}.
Use built-in moveTo for walking to markers: one initial settling tick, then one grid cell per tick toward the destination. A new move replaces the destination. Activation while moving is allowed only if the current position meets the zone condition. Movement itself is not activation.
Initially case is locked, flags started=false and alarm=false. Timer countdown has capacity 400 ticks and initial remaining time 0. Runtime is 20 ticks per second. The sensor input is action activate targeting sensorA or sensorB.
The first valid sensorA activation while started=false, alarm=false and case locked sets started=true and starts countdown for 400 ticks (20 seconds). Later sensorA activations do not restart it.
sensorB activation before sensorA has no effect and is not remembered. A valid sensorB activation while started=true, alarm=false, case locked and countdown running unlocks case and stops countdown.
Countdown expiry while case is locked sets alarm=true and leaves case locked. Expiry wins over sensorB activation on the same tick. After alarm, case cannot unlock. After unlocking, further activations have no effect.
Goal: case unlocked and alarm=false. No other mechanisms or reset actions. Appearance is schematic.
