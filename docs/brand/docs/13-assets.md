## Share output (product feature, generated per game)
These are generated by the app when a user shares; they are part of the product, not marketing.
- **Dare card / OG image** 1200×630: cream background, frozen game frame left (rounded xl, ink border), right: game title (display-m), the time to beat in a sun badge, "Can you beat it?" and the lockup bottom-right. Used as `og:image` for dare links.
- **Story clip** 1080×1920, ≤ 15s: gameplay recording, HUD visible, end card 1.5s with time + "Say it. Play it." + mark. Mark watermark bottom-right, 72px, always 100% opacity on a small paper pill with ink border (never a faded overlay).
- Link format: `sayplay.[tld]/g/{gameId}?dare={runId}` (placeholder — confirm domain).

## Exporting new assets
- SVG: outline all text, colours from tokens only, no embedded fonts or raster images.
- PNG: sRGB, @2x and @3x for app use.
- Filenames: kebab-case, no spaces.
