# 01 · Brand foundations

## What Sayplay is
A sentence becomes a playable 3D mini-game in seconds, and every game is made to be passed to a friend. The product loop is **Say → Play → Dare → Remix**. Every screen should push the user one step around that loop.

## Name & tagline
- Name: **sayplay** — always lowercase in the logo. In running text write "Sayplay" (capital S) at the start of a sentence and in legal copy.
- Tagline: **Say it. Play it.** Two short sentences, full stops, no exclamation marks.

## Personality
| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Chunky, tactile, toy-like | Glassy, techy, "AI-magic" sparkles |
| Cheeky, quick, a bit smug when you win | Sarcastic about the user, mean |
| Confident and simple | Corporate, apologetic, over-explained |
| Made by people for friends | A generic "create with AI" tool |

## The six rules
1. **Ink outlines everything.** 2.5px ink borders keep the UI readable over any generated world.
2. **Shadows are hard, never blurred.** Solid ink offsets of 3, 5 or 7px.
3. **Pressing sinks the button.** The signature interaction.
4. **Tilt is a treat.** Only idea cards (±1.5°), world tags (−4°) and celebration badges (±6°).
5. **One loud colour per screen.** Tomato = the primary action. Sun supports. The rest is cream, paper, ink.
6. **Plain, cheeky words.** See `11-voice-copy.md`.

## What to avoid (looks templated / "AI-made")
- Gradient washes, glassmorphism, blurred drop shadows, glow effects.
- Neon, purple, violet, "AI sparkle" icons (✨).
- Random rotation on buttons, inputs, tabs or text blocks.
- ALL-CAPS tracked-out eyebrow labels over every heading.
- Emoji used as icons.
